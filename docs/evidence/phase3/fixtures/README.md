Phase 3 media fixtures.

All three are generated by scripts/verify/make-fixtures.mjs with headless
Chromium. They are synthetic signage, not photographs of real businesses, and
they contain no personal data.

- menu-malaysia.png  — a Malaysian nasi lemak stall menu: dish names, prices,
  a spice note, and the local term "bungkus" (takeaway).
- campus-singapore.png — a Singapore campus notice: English plus Malay and
  Mandarin, with a date and a room number.
- mixed-chat.png — a screenshot-style group chat mixing English with Malay,
  Mandarin and Vietnamese, which is what a real student actually receives.
