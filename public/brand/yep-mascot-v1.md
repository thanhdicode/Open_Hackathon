# Yep — YapYep mascot concept v1

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, 2026-09-17. Asset: `yep-mascot-v1.png`.

Yep is a friendly otter student buddy. Cream body, dark outline, cobalt crossbody satchel, waving pose. The curled tail suggests conversation. This is a proposed mascot identity, not a replacement for the frozen product design system.

YapYep helps ASEAN students interpret context, rehearse conversations, and adapt to student life. A supportive peer fits this purpose. The palette follows the existing cream/neutral canvas and cobalt accent direction without assigning a national identity.

Suggested placements: welcome hero at 160–220px, sign-in header at 80–112px, onboarding completion at 140–180px. At very small icon sizes, a dedicated simplified face mark should be generated rather than shrinking the full body. This delivery adds the asset; it does not change application screens.
